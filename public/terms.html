Terms of use

Christ NCR Forum · last updated 15 October 2026

Christ NCR Forum is an independent, student-run board for raising problems at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Delhi NCR. It is not run by or affiliated with the university. By signing in you agree to these terms.

Who can use it

Anyone can read the board. Posting, voting, flagging and commenting need a christuniversity.in account, and the account must be your own.

What you can post

  • Real problems with the college: classes, facilities, events, processes and so on.
  • Describe roles and offices, not individuals. Posts that name or clearly identify a person are blocked.
  • No abuse, threats, harassment, hate, spam, advertising or other people's private information.
  • For anything about your safety, use the private Safety category. If you are in danger, contact campus security or emergency services first.

Moderation

Posts are checked automatically before they go up. Other students can flag posts; a post flagged by three people is hidden until an admin reviews it. Admins can reject, hide or delete posts that break these terms, and can see who posted. See the privacy policy for details.

No guarantees

We try to keep the board running and accurate, but it is provided as is. Posting here is not a formal grievance with the university, and we can't promise any issue will be fixed.

Changes and contact
