Privacy policy

Christ NCR Forum · last updated 15 October 2026

Christ NCR Forum is an independent, student-run complaint board for students of CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Delhi NCR. It is not an official university service. This page explains what we collect and who can see it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your college email address, and the name and course read from it (or the name you type in, if it can't be read). You also give your nationality on first sign-in.
  • What you post: complaints, comments, upvotes, flags and check-in answers, with the time of each.
  • Contact form: the name, contact details and message you choose to send.

We use Google sign-in only to confirm your email address. We do not read your Gmail, Drive or anything else in your Google account.

Who can see what

  • Other students and visitors see complaints and comments with no name, email or course.
  • The forum's admins can see who posted, voted or flagged, so they can follow up and stop abuse. Every time an admin views identities it is logged.
  • Complaints in the Safety category, and any post you mark private, are visible only to admins.

Automated checks

When you post, the text of your complaint is sent to an AI service (Google Gemini) to suggest a title and category, spot duplicates, and block posts that name individuals or are abusive. Your name and email are not sent.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase in Mumbai, India. The site is served by Cloudflare. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

You can delete any of your complaints yourself from the Mine tab. To delete your whole account, write to us using the contact details below.
